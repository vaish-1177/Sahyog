# Asset notes

Resource photos are bundled for dependable local demonstrations. See SOURCES.json for original image URLs and the corrected 3D-printer photo attribution. They illustrate resource categories; they are not verified photos of listed fictional institutions or exact hardware models.

The retained image set originated in the user’s source. Unsplash license information: https://unsplash.com/license. The corrected 3D-printer image is by Efe Yağız Soysal: https://unsplash.com/photos/SU2yTv4Hwis. No endorsement by the photographer or manufacturer is implied.

Inter and Manrope were downloaded from Google Fonts. Their SIL Open Font License notices are in fonts/INTER-OFL.txt and fonts/MANROPE-OFL.txt. Local TTF files are used without modification.

Leaflet 1.9.4 is bundled under vendor/ with its license. Tiles remain an external OpenStreetMap service and retain visible attribution. Default Leaflet image-marker assets are not used; this application uses vector circle markers.

All outline icons and the connected-resource brand motif are inline SVG/CSS created for this interface. Uploaded user demo photos are not shipped here; they stay in the browser’s IndexedDB.
